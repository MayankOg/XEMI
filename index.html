Case Studies

Real Teams. Real Trade. Real Outcomes.

Importer

Mid-sized pharmaceutical manufacturer, Mumbai · 80+ import shipments/month across 6 origins

Demurrage charges hitting ₹4–6 lakh every month. Document errors discovered only after containers reached port. Ops team spending 2 hours each morning across five portals assembling an already-outdated picture.

—

XEMI's AI caught discrepancies before filing. Free-day countdowns and demurrage alerts gave the team time to act. ₹0 avoidable demurrage in six months following go-live.

CHA Firm

Licensed customs house agent, JNPT · Managing 40+ importer accounts, 300+ jobs/month

Every job took 75 minutes of operator time — data entry, HSN lookup, eSanchit upload, ICEGATE navigation. Could not onboard new clients without hiring. Flat file rejections at 5pm daily.

—

XEMI automated job creation, HSN classification, and ICEGATE filing. The same job now takes 11 minutes. One operator handles what previously required three.

Exporter

Chemical exporter, Ahmedabad · 120+ Shipping Bills/month · Active EPCG and RoDTEP obligations

Duty drawback filed late or not at all. EPCG utilization tracked on a spreadsheet three weeks behind. A compliance notice from DGFT revealed how exposed the team was.

—

XEMI identified eligible drawbacks on every shipment automatically. ₹38 lakh in duty drawback recovered in the first four months. Zero DGFT compliance breaches since.

Freight Forwarder

Mid-market freight forwarding firm, Delhi · 200+ active shipments/month across India-Middle East & India-Europe lanes

90 minutes every morning pulling status from eight carrier portals. Clients calling constantly for updates. CHA coordination entirely over WhatsApp — no audit trail, no structure.

—

Consolidated carrier data into one live dashboard. Client self-serve visibility portals. Morning data assembly dropped from 90 minutes to under 10. Client status calls stopped within the first month.
